SPORTS ROUNDUP

 
                               

      

      
 
 
LPGA TOURNAMENT


KATHY WHITWORTH CAPTURED

THE $200,000 LPGA TOURNAMENT IN

PORTSMOUTH, VIRGINIA, TODAY.

WHITWORTH SHOT A 72, FINISHING

ONE STROKE AHEAD OF DEFENDING

CHAMPION AMY ALCOTT.



       
 
 
NELSON CLASSIC


BOB EASTWOOD WON THE BYRON

NELSON GOLF CLASSIC TODAY BY

DEFEATING PAYNE STEWART ON

THE FIRST HOLE OF SUDDEN-DEATH

PLAY.


TOM WATSON AND MAC O'GRADY TIED

FOR THIRD PLACE IN

THE TOURNAMENT.



     
 
 
GOLF INVITATIONAL


DON JANUARY WON THE DOMINION

SENIORS INVITATIONAL TODAY TO

FINISH TWO STROKES AHEAD OF GAY

BREWER.


WITH THE WIN, JANUARY BECAME

THE FIRST PLAYER TO TOP 

THE $900,000 MARK IN CAREER

WINNINGS ON THE PGA SENIORS

TOUR.



        
 
 
MEN'S TENNIS


IVAN LENDL WON AGAINST JOHN

McENROE TODAY 6-3, 6-3 IN

THE TITLE MATCH OF

THE TOURNAMENT OF CHAMPIONS

IN FOREST HILLS, NEW YORK.


LENDL'S VICTORY WAS WORTH

$80,000.  McENROE PICKED UP

A $40,000 CHECK FOR HIS PLAY.



      
 
 
GOLF TOURNAMENT


BRITAIN'S DAVID RUSSELL WON HIS

FIRST PRO GOLF TITLE TODAY WITH

A ONE-STROKE VICTORY IN

A $135,000 TOURNAMENT IN LEEDS,

ENGLAND.


FORMER UNITED STATES AMATEUR

CHAMPION NATHANIEL CROSBY

FINISHED IN A TIE FOR 18th

PLACE.



         
 
 
SWIM MEET


STEFAN VOLERY AND SWISS TEAMMATE

DANO HALSALL FINISHED IN FIRST

AND SECOND PLACES RESPECTIVELY

IN THE 100-METER FREESTYLE AT

TODAY'S INTERNATIONAL SWIM MEET

IN ROME.


OLYMPIAN GIANNI MINERVINI POSTED

THE WORLD'S SECOND-BEST TIME

THIS SEASON IN THE 100-METER

BREASTSTROKE.



         
 
 
NBA DRAFT


NATIONAL BASKETBALL COMMISSIONER

DAVID STERN DREW ENVELOPES TODAY

TO DETERMINE THE FIRST SEVEN

TEAMS ELIGIBLE TO PICK IN NEXT

MONTH'S COLLEGE DRAFT. 

THE FIRST PICK IN THE JUNE 18th

DRAFT GOES TO THE NEW YORK

KNICKS.


THE KNICKS HAD THE NBA'S

THIRD-WORST RECORD LAST SEASON,

WINNING ONLY 24 GAMES.  


THE SECOND PICK GOES TO INDIANA,

FOLLOWED BY THE LOS ANGELES

CLIPPERS, SEATTLE, ATLANTA,

SACRAMENTO, AND GOLDEN STATE.



  
 
 
CURCI APPOINTED DIRECTOR


FRAN CURCI TODAY WAS NAMED 

ATHLETIC DIRECTOR AT

THE UNIVERSITY OF TAMPA.


CURCI WAS TAMPA'S HEAD FOOTBALL 

COACH FOR THREE SEASONS BEORE

TAKING THE HEAD COACHING JOB AT

THE UNIVERSITY OF MIAMI IN 1971.



  
 
 
STRAWBERRY OUT OF LINEUP


THE NEW YORK METS PLACED

1983 ROOKIE OF THE YEAR DARRYL

STRAWBERRY ON THE 15-DAY

DISABLED LIST BECAUSE OF A TORN

LIGAMENT BETWEEN THE THUMB AND

INDEX FINGER ON HIS RIGHT HAND.


STRAWBERRY WAS INJURED WHILE

MAKING A DIVING CATCH IN

YESTERDAY'S GAME AGAINST

THE PHILADELPHIA PHILLIES.
