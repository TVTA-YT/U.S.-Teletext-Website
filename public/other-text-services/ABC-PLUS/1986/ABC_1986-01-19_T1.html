MASTERS TENNIS TOURNAMENT


IVAN LENDL BEAT BORIS BECKER

TODAY IN THE MASTERS TENNIS

TOURNAMENT AT MADISON SQUARE

GARDEN IN NEW YORK.  LENDL WON

IN STRAIGHT SETS, 6-2, 7-6, 6-3.


LENDL HAS WON THE MASTERS

TOURNAMENT THREE CONSECUTIVE

TIMES.  HE IS RANKED AT THE TOP

OF THE MEN'S PROFESSIONAL TENNIS

LADDER.



   
 
 
HOPE CHRYSLER CLASSIC


DONNIE HAMMOND TODAY WON

THE BOB HOPE CHRYSLER CLASSIC ON

THE MEN'S GOLF CIRCUIT.  HE

DEFEATED JOHN COOK ON THE FIRST

HOLE OF A SUDDEN-DEATH PLAYOFF.


HAMMOND TAKES HOME $108,000 FOR

HIS FIRST PGA TITLE.



        
 
 
MEN'S SLALOM


LIECHTENSTEIN'S PAUL FROMMELT

TODAY WON A MEN'S WORLD SKI CUP

SLALOM EVENT IN AUSTRIA.


FROMMELT RECORDED HIS BEST

SLALOM FINISH OF THE SEASON IN

THE MEET.  HE EDGED OUT

SECOND-PLACE FINISHER INGEMAR

STENMARK BY ONE SECOND.



        
 
 
GIANT SLALOM


VRENI SCHNEIDER WON THE WOMEN'S

GIANT SLALOM SKI EVENT TODAY IN

SOUTHERN GERMANY.


SCHNEIDER HAD A TIME OF 2:14.33.

MICHAELA GERG OF WEST GERMANY

PLACED SECOND.


SCHNEIDER NOW IS THIRD IN

THE OVERALL WORLD CUP STANDINGS

BEHIND MARIA WALLISER AND ERIKA

HESS.



 
 
STABLE FIRE AT BELMONT PARK


A FIRE THAT MAY HAVE STARTED IN

A BELMONT PARK HAYSTACK TODAY

KILLED AT LEAST 45 RACEHORSES

AND CAUSED AN ESTIMATED

$10 MILLION IN DAMAGE IN

BELMONT, NEW YORK.


RACETRACK OFFICIALS SAID

THE STABLE'S SPRINKLER SYSTEM

AT THE RACETRACK HAD NOT BEEN

REPAIRED AFTER BURSTING BECAUSE

OF WEATHER CONDITIONS LAST

THURSDAY.
