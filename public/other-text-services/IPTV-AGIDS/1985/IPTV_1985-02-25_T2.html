   More than one-thousand 

Beluga whales swam to freedom

   They can thank a Soviet 

icebreaker, which slashed a 

channel to the open sea for 

their escape route. 

   The Soviet news agency Tass 

says the whales swam after the 

icebreaker ''Moscow'' tore a 20 

nautical-mile path through the 

ice from the Semyavin 
 
(sem-

YAH'-veen)
 

 Strait to the open sea. 

   Said Tass  ''It was as if 

the animals understood that the 

ship was their salvation. They 

literally swam after its stern, 

getting out of the icy trap.'' 


   The effort climaxed a two-

week rescue operation.

environmental organization
