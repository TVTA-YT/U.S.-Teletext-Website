Affiliates That Altered VBI

Not every affiliate appreciated teletext...

As discussed in a section in the Archiving Teletext page, not every broadcast affiliate is alike. Unfortunately, this meant that there were some affiliates that altered their VBI; in this case, CBS's ExtraVision and NBC's NBC Teletext services were either partially passed through the VBI or completely omitted from the VBI. While you cancan get some results from decoding partial a teletext stream (though not the entire data), most (or all) of the pages will be corrupted.

Below is a list of broadcast affiliates that altered their VBI, which affected the teletext stream.

Affiliate City/State Network Status Notes
WHAS Louisville, KY CBS Partial Determination made based on 1988 recording.
WJKB Detroit, MI CBS Omitted Based on 1980s recording.
WKYT Lexington, KY CBS Partial Determination made based on recording from August 27, 1988.
WMAR Baltimore, MD CBS Omitted Determination made based on recording from May 1983.
WRC-TV Washington, D.C. NBC Partial Determination made based on recording from June 16, 1983.
