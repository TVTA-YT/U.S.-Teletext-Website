Affiliates That Altered VBI

Not every affiliate appreciated teletext...

As discussed in a section in the Archiving Teletext page, not every broadcast affiliate is alike. Unfortunately, this meant that there were some affiliates that altered their VBI; in this case, CBS's ExtraVision and NBC's NBC Teletext services were either partially passed through the VBI or completely omitted from the VBI. While you cancan recover some pages from a partial set of teletext lines, the complete magazine will likely not be recoverable.

Below is a list of broadcast affiliates that altered their VBI, which affected the teletext stream. Scroll the tables to view more affiliates (if more than 4 are listed).

Partial Teletext

Affiliate City/State Network Status Source
KMTV Omaha, NE CBS Partial Recording from September 1987.
WHAS Louisville, KY CBS Partial Multiple recordings from 1987-1990.
WRC-TV Washington, D.C. NBC Partial Recording from 05/16/1983.

Omitted Teletext

Affiliate City/State Network Status Source
KOLD Tuscon, AZ CBS Omitted Recording from around 1984.
KTSP Phoenix, AZ CBS Omitted Recording from around Dec 1991.
KOLN Lincoln & Grand Island, NE CBS Omitted Recording from around 1989.
WDBJ Roanoke, VA CBS Omitted Recording from 11/28/1991.
WJBK Detroit, MI CBS Omitted Multiple late 1988 recordings.
WJW-TV Cleveland, OH CBS Omitted Recording from December 1987.
WKYC Cleveland, OH NBC Omitted* Recording from 05/16/1983.
WKYT Lexington, KY CBS Partial Recording from 08/27/1988.
WMAR Baltimore, MD NBC Omitted Recording from 05/16/1983.
WTCN Palm Beach, FL NBC Omitted Recording from Aug 1983.

*Recordings from WKYC from after October 1983 do include the full teletext stream in the VBI. It is currently unknown when WKYC started passing through NBC Teletext in their VBI.
