Privacy Policy

Updated October 30, 2026

1. Who We Are

The North American Teletext Archive (NATA) (hereafter "we," "us," "our," or the "Site") is an independent, non-commercial archival project dedicated to preserving and showcasing decoded pages from North American teletext services such as CBS ExtraVision, NBC Teletext, and Electra (in the U.S.) and Edutel (in Canada), in addition to pages from other text services such as Kentucky Educational Television's AgText. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any of the broadcast networks referenced on this Site. We do not make any income from this website.

4. Information You Provide Directly

If you use our Contact or Submissions pages to send us a message or submit teletext samples, we collect whatever information you choose to include (such as your name, email address, message content, sample details, and uploaded files). These forms are processed by Formspree, a form-processing service, which delivers the final form to us. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry or process your submission, and is not shared with third parties except as needed to fulfill that purpose (for example, a form-processing service, if one is used).

The name you enter into the Submission form (or your contributor name if signed in) may be shown publicly in the Site as the "Recovered By" credit for samples we add.

5. Contributor Accounts

Creating an account is optional; you can still submit samples without an account. If you choose to create one, your contributor name is automatically filled in on the Submissions form. Signing in also allows you to view your submissions and credited samples on the My Submissions page. Signing in uses a one-time link sent to your email address; there is no password to create or remember.

6. How We Use Collected Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Understand which archived teletext services and pages get the most interest
  • Identify broken links or technical issues
  • Prioritize future archiving efforts based on what visitors seem to care about
  • Review submissions and credit contributors for the samples they provide
  • Sign contributors in and show them their own submissions

We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data. We do not use collected data for advertising or ad targeting, and we have no ability to do so even if we wanted to (which we would never do anyway) — GoatCounter simply doesn't collect the kind of identifying data that would make that possible. Contributor email addresses are used only for signing in.

7. Your Choices

Umami uses no cookies or tracking identifiers stored in your browser, so there is no consent to manage or opt out of. If you'd prefer not to be counted in aggregate page-view statistics at all, using a browser extension that blocks third-party scripts (such as uBlock Origin) will prevent the Umami script from loading.

If you have a contributor account, you can:

  • Sign out at any time, which ends your session and removes the sign-in cookie
  • Ask us to change your contributor name
  • Ask us to delete your account

To make any of these requests, contact us using the email address used for your account.

8. Data Retention

  • Aggregate page-view counts are retained indefinitely for historical comparison (e.g., "this page has had X views since launch"), but since no individual visitor is ever identified, there is no personal data to delete or expire.
  • Analytics collected with GoatCounter (August 2026 - November 1, 2026) was likewise aggregate and non-identifying, and is kept only for historical comparison.
  • Messages submitted through our contact or submissions forms are retained only as long as necessary to respond to or process them, or as required by applicable law.
  • Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and are deleted once used or expired.
  • Sign-in sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out, and from there are deleted.
  • Contributor account and submission details are kept until you ask us to delete them. Deleting your account removes your email address, sessions, and submission details. "Recovered By" credits on samples already added to the Site remain part of the submission records unless you ask us to change them.
