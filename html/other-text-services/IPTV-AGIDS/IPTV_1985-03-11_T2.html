 Iowa Morning Sports Roundup 

 
 
(bkc-ncaa-iowa)
 


 

   Both the Iowa and Iowa State 

basketball teams were given a 

new lease on life yesterday 

when they each got one of the 

64 coveted berths in the N-C-

Double-A Basketball Tournament. 

For Iowa State, it is the 

team's first N-C-Double-A post-

season tournament berth in 41 

years. For Iowa, it is the 

first tournament bid under 

second-year coach George 

Raveling. The Hawkeyes did not 

receive a bid in 1984 for the 

first time in five years. 

 

   The Hawkeyes are scheduled 

to take on Arkansas at 2:37 p-m 

 
(Mountain time)
 

 Thursday in Salt Lake City. 

Iowa is seeded eighth in the 

West Regional and Arkansas is 

seeded ninth. 

 

   The Cyclones will play Ohio 

State at 9:37 p-m Thursday in 

Tulsa, Oklahoma. The Cyclones 

are seeded 13th in the Midwest 

Regional and Ohio State is 

seeded fourth. 

 

   Iowa has lost six of its 

last eight games and Coach 

Raveling says he was 

tremendously relieved when he 

heard the Hawkeyes had secured 

a berth. He says the Hawkeyes 

will now have a chance to 

redeem themselves. They beat 

Arkansas 71-52 in the Rainbow 

Classic in December. Raveling 

says he has mixed emotions 

about the pairing because of 

what he said is the difficulty 

of beating the same team twice. 


 

   Iowa State Coach Johnny Orr 

says that for him, the 

tournament bid is a dream come 

true. He praised his team's 

opponent... but says he thinks 

the Cyclones can beat the 

Buckeyes. Both teams finished 

third in their conferences 

this season. Orr says he is 

surprised the Cyclones were 

not seeded a little higher in 

the rankings after their 

outstanding performance in the 

Big Eight tournament this 

weekend.
