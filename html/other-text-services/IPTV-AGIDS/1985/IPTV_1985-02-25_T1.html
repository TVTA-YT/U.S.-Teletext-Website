     PRICE PROTECTION TAKEN

================================

  Cash price protection of 3-5 

cnts on soybeans and 1-2 cnts 

on corn was taken this morning 

by merchandisers across the 

Midwest on ideas futures prices

would open lower under pressure

from weaker outside markets and

from increased selling.

  Protection was widespread,with

increased elevator reselling

noted in the Eastern production

belt.
