BREAKING: CBS ExtraVision did not shut down in 1988

You may have read before from various places that ExtraVision shut down in 1988. However, what if I were to say that wasn't the case? Have a look for yourself...

Those are recently decoded pages that come from a VHS recording from February 28, 1990; you can even see the full date in one of the photos. Let me add that there are a lot of decoding issues with these pages. Part of that is likely due to current limitations with Decode-Orc. As I've stated elsewhere, the software is still in in development, so there may likely be less of those issues as development progresses. Another possible issue is that the videotape these pages were sourced from was recorded in EP/SLP mode, which can make obtaining cleaner decodes more difficult due to the lower quality.

Anyway, this was quite a development because, based on currently available information, no source has stated that ExtraVision lasted past 1988.


August 13, 2026

NBC Teletext from Late 1985

Sticking with the theme of "sources say one thing but the evidence says otherwise," we have an example of NBC Teletext from October 1985. Various sources have stated that NBC's service shut down in January 1985.

Again, there are some errors, but hopefully those will be resolved over time.

NBC's service is a but of an oddity. According to someone capable of transferring VHS via FM RF, a VHS tape with content recorded in mid-January 1985 did not contain teletext data in thw VBI. However, as you can see above, a VHS tape with content recorded in October 1985 did contain teletext. As of now, there's no clear reason as to why it seems NBC shut down the service, then brought it back We also don't know exactly when NBC restored the service or when they shut it down for good. Hopefully, as more samples are decoded, we will have a clearer answer.
